Our Story

Teti Tours was founded in 2021 with a simple goal: to provide travelers visiting Egypt with high-quality, comfortable, and reliable travel services. What started as a one-person operation has grown into a dedicated team working together to support our guests throughout their journey in Egypt.

What makes Teti Tours different is our focus on the complete customer experience. We provide high-quality services using modern vehicles and experienced, professional guides, while making sure every guest feels comfortable, safe, and happy throughout their trip. For us, customer satisfaction is not just part of the service — it is our highest priority.

Today, our team is available 24 hours a day to assist our customers, from the moment they arrive in Egypt until the end of their journey.
