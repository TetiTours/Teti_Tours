Our Story

Teti Tour was founded by a small team of Egypt-based guides and drivers who wanted to offer something different from the typical group tour: private, unhurried journeys that let travelers actually experience Egypt, not just check landmarks off a list.

Named after King Teti, the founder of Egypt's Sixth Dynasty, our mission is simple — to walk our guests through thousands of years of history with the same care and attention we'd want for our own family, from the moment they land to the moment they say goodbye to the Nile.

Every itinerary is planned around comfort, safety, and genuine local knowledge, whether it's a first-time trip to the Pyramids of Giza or a deeper dive into Saqqara, Dahshur, and beyond.
