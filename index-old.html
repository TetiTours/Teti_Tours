FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before You Book

Here are answers to some common questions about our tours and transfers.

Do you offer private airport transfers?

Yes. Teti Tour offers private transfers to or from Cairo International Airport and Sphinx International Airport.

Can I choose my pickup time?

Yes. The booking form lets you enter your preferred pickup time. Final arrangements can then be confirmed with Teti Tour on WhatsApp.

Can I book a private Cairo and Giza tour?

Yes. You can choose from several private Cairo & Giza experiences, including the Giza Pyramids, Grand Egyptian Museum, Saqqara, Dahshur, Memphis and Nile experiences.

Do you offer day trips outside Cairo?

Yes. Our current day-trip options include Fayoum and Alexandria.

How do I confirm my booking?

After completing the booking form, your trip details are prepared in WhatsApp. You can then communicate with Teti Tour to confirm the booking details.
