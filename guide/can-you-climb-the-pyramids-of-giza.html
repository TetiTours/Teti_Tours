Can You Climb the Pyramids of Giza?

4 min read

Many visitors ask whether they can climb the Pyramids of Giza and how long it takes. Here is the honest answer, and what you can do instead to get a great experience.

Quick answer: You cannot climb the Pyramids of Giza. It is not allowed and it is not safe. You can still get very close, go inside with an extra ticket, and see them from the best viewpoints.

Why You Cannot Climb Them

  • It is prohibited. Climbing the outside of the pyramids is not permitted.
  • It is dangerous. The stones are large, uneven and worn, and falls have happened.
  • It protects the monuments. Thousands of visitors every day would damage the stone.

Because of this, there is no "time it takes to climb" for visitors. If someone offers to take you up for a fee, say no.

What You Can Do Instead

  • Walk around the base. Standing beside the huge blocks shows you the scale much better than a photo.
  • Go inside a pyramid. Entry to the Great Pyramid needs a separate ticket. The passages are narrow, steep and warm, so it is not for everyone.
  • Visit the panoramic viewpoint. From here you can see all the main pyramids together for classic photos.
  • Meet the Sphinx. It sits close to the pyramids, and most visits include it.
  • Add the Grand Egyptian Museum. It is close by, and the two combine well in one day.
  • See the Pyramids at sunset. The desert camp is a quiet way to enjoy the view and dinner afterwards.

How Long Should You Plan?

Most visitors spend around half a day at the plateau. For a detailed plan, read how long to spend at the Giza Pyramids.

Tips for the Visit

  • Go early, when it is cooler and quieter.
  • Wear a hat, sunglasses and comfortable shoes, and bring water.
  • Decide in advance if you want to go inside, as it needs an extra ticket.
  • Book a private guide, so you reach the best viewpoints without wasting time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you climb the Pyramids of Giza?

No. Climbing the pyramids is not allowed, and it is not safe. You can walk around them, photograph them and, with an extra ticket, go inside.

Can you go inside the Great Pyramid?

Yes, with a separate ticket. The passages are narrow, steep and warm, so it is not ideal for anyone who dislikes tight spaces. Which interiors are open can change, so check on the day.

What is the best way to see the Pyramids if I cannot climb them?

Visit the viewpoints on the plateau, walk to the bases of the pyramids, and see the Pyramids from the desert at sunset. A private guide helps you find the best spots.
