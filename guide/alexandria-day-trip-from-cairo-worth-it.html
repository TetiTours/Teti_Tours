Alexandria Day Trip from Cairo: Is It Worth It?

4 min read

Alexandria is the Mediterranean city Alexander the Great founded, and it feels very different from Cairo. But it is also a long drive away, so many travelers ask whether a day trip is worth it. Here is an honest answer.

The Short Answer

Yes, if you have a spare day and want something beyond the pyramids. You get sea air, Greek and Roman history and a very different atmosphere from Cairo. If you only have two or three days in Egypt, spend them on Giza, Saqqara and the museums first.

How Long Is the Drive?

Alexandria is roughly 2.5 to 3 hours each way from Cairo by road, depending on traffic and where you start. Expect a long day, so an early start makes a big difference. A private car lets you rest on the way and stop when you need to.

What You Can See in One Day

  • The Catacombs of Kom El Shoqafa: an underground burial site that mixes Egyptian, Greek and Roman styles in one place.
  • Qaitbay Citadel: a 15th-century fortress on the sea, built on the site of the ancient Lighthouse of Alexandria, one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World.
  • The Bibliotheca Alexandrina: a striking modern library built as a tribute to the famous ancient Library of Alexandria.
  • The corniche: the seafront road, where a relaxed seafood lunch is a classic part of the day.

Who Is It For?

  • Good fit: travelers with four or more days in Egypt, history lovers, and anyone who wants a break from Cairo's crowds and noise.
  • Maybe skip it: if your time is short, if you dislike long drives, or if you have not yet seen the main sights in Cairo and Giza.

Tips for a Smooth Day

  • Leave early. You will have more time in the city and avoid the worst of the traffic.
  • Wear comfortable shoes. There is a fair amount of walking, including steps at the catacombs.
  • Bring a light layer. The sea breeze can be cool, especially in winter.
  • Expect crowds in summer. Egyptians head to the coast in summer, so weekends can be busy.
  • Go private if you can. A private tour lets you set the pace, skip what does not interest you and take your time over lunch.

Our Advice

Treat Alexandria as a full-day trip, not a quick add-on. If you have the time, it is a memorable change of scene and a good way to see a different side of Egypt.
