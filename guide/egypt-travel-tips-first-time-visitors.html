Egypt Travel Tips for First-Time Visitors

6 min read

Egypt is a rewarding destination, and a little preparation makes the first visit much smoother. These are the practical tips most first-time visitors find useful.

Quick answer: Plan your trip for October to April, dress modestly and comfortably, start sightseeing early, carry some small cash for tips and small purchases, and consider a private driver and guide to make getting around simple. Check visa and entry rules before you travel, as they can change.

Check Entry Requirements

Visa rules depend on your nationality and can change, so check the current requirements with the official Egyptian authorities or your embassy before you book. Make sure your passport is valid for the period required by the rules.

Choose the Right Season

The most comfortable months are October to April. Summer is hot, so plan early starts. See our guide on the best time to visit Egypt for details.

What to Wear

  • Light, breathable clothes that cover shoulders and knees are comfortable and respectful, especially at mosques and in local areas.
  • Comfortable walking shoes, since many sites involve sand, steps and uneven ground.
  • A hat, sunglasses and sunscreen.
  • A light jacket for winter evenings and the desert.
  • A scarf, useful for sun, dust or visiting mosques.

Money and Tipping

The local currency is the Egyptian pound. Carry some small notes, since tipping is customary for guides, drivers, restaurant staff and many small services, and small notes are not always easy to change. Cards are accepted in many hotels and restaurants, while smaller shops and markets often prefer cash. It helps to agree on prices before buying anything or using a service that has no fixed price.

Getting Around

Cairo traffic can be heavy, and distances between sites add up. Many visitors choose a private driver for the day, which avoids negotiating rides and saves time. For airport arrivals, see our guide on how to get from Cairo Airport to Giza.

Plan Your Days Realistically

Egypt rewards early starts. The Giza Pyramids, Saqqara and the museums are best visited in the morning, when it is cooler and quieter. Avoid packing too many sites into one day. Our guides on how long to spend at the Giza Pyramids and what to see in Cairo in one day can help.

Tickets and Entry

Entrance tickets to major sites are separate from transport. When booking a tour, check whether tickets are included, and whether special areas, such as going inside a pyramid, cost extra. Keep your passport or a copy with you if asked.

Staying Healthy

  • Drink bottled or filtered water.
  • Carry water on sightseeing days, especially in warm months.
  • Take sun protection seriously, even in winter.
  • Pace yourself: heat, walking and long days can be tiring.

Respecting Local Culture

Egyptians are known for their hospitality. Polite greetings, modest dress at religious sites and asking before photographing people go a long way. During Ramadan, avoid eating or drinking openly in public during the day out of courtesy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time of year for a first visit to Egypt?

October to April offers the most comfortable weather, with the busiest period in December and January.

What should I wear in Egypt?

Light, breathable clothing that covers shoulders and knees, comfortable shoes, a hat and sunscreen, plus a light jacket for winter evenings.

Do I need cash in Egypt?

Yes, carry some small Egyptian pound notes for tips and small purchases. Cards are accepted in many hotels and restaurants.

Is it easy to get around Cairo?

Traffic can be heavy, so many travellers book a private driver for sightseeing days and airport transfers.

How many days do I need in Cairo?

Many travellers spend three to four days to see the Pyramids, Saqqara, the museums and Islamic Cairo without rushing.

Should I tip in Egypt?

Tipping is customary for guides, drivers and service staff. Carry small notes so it is easy to do.
