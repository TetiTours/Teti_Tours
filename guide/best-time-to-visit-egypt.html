Best Time to Visit Egypt: Weather, Crowds and Seasons

6 min read

Egypt can be visited all year, but the season you pick changes how comfortable your days are, how crowded the sites feel and what you can do. Here is a simple guide to help you choose the right time for your trip.

Quick answer: The best time to visit Egypt is from October to April, when days are warm to mild and comfortable for sightseeing. December and January are the busiest months, while October, November, March and April offer a good balance of weather and fewer crowds. Summer is hot, but still possible with early starts.

Quick Overview by Season

  • Autumn (October to November): warm, sunny days and pleasant evenings. One of the best times to visit.
  • Winter (December to February): mild days and cool nights. Peak season, so popular sites are busier.
  • Spring (March to April): warm and comfortable, though occasional dusty winds can blow in.
  • Summer (May to September): hot and dry in Cairo and Upper Egypt, with long daylight hours.

October and November

Cairo cools down after the summer, with sunny days and comfortable evenings. It is a great time for the Giza Pyramids, the Saqqara area and the Egyptian Museum area, and the desert is pleasant for a day trip. Visitor numbers build up through November ahead of the winter peak.

December to February

This is the classic Egypt season, with mild daytime weather that suits long days of walking at the pyramids and in Islamic Cairo. Mornings and evenings can be cool, so bring a light jacket. Expect more visitors, especially around Christmas and New Year, so booking early helps. If you plan to camp in the White Desert, note that desert nights in winter can be cold, so pack warm layers. Our guide on the White Desert day trip or overnight camping has more detail.

March and April

Spring brings warm weather, longer days and good light for photos. Occasionally, spring winds carry dust across Cairo, which can make some days hazy for a short time. It is still a very good time to visit, with fewer crowds than mid-winter.

May to September

Summer in Cairo is hot, and in Luxor and Aswan it is hotter still. You can still enjoy Egypt with some planning: start sightseeing early in the morning, rest during the middle of the day, and keep water with you. Many sites are quieter, and indoor stops such as museums and air-conditioned vehicles make the heat easier. Evening activities like a Nile dinner cruise or a felucca ride are especially pleasant once the sun goes down. The north coast and Alexandria are breezier than Cairo, so they suit summer days well.

Ramadan and Holidays

Ramadan moves about eleven days earlier each year, because it follows the Islamic calendar. During the month, daily life shifts: some restaurants open later in the day, and evenings become lively after sunset. Major sites usually stay open, though hours can change. Eid holidays after Ramadan bring local travellers to popular places, so expect busier roads and attractions on those days. It is worth checking the dates for the year you travel.

Best Time for Different Plans

  • Pyramids and Cairo sightseeing: October to April. See how long you need at the Giza Pyramids to plan your day.
  • White and Black Desert: October to March for comfortable daytime weather, with warm clothing for the night.
  • Alexandria: a good day trip all year, and especially pleasant in spring and early summer.
  • Nile cruise or felucca ride: any time, with the best evenings from autumn to spring.
  • Fewer crowds: late October, November, March or April, and the summer months if you can handle the heat.

Tips Whatever the Season

  • Start early. Mornings are cooler and quieter at the main sites.
  • Wear light, comfortable clothes and a hat, plus sunscreen and comfortable shoes.
  • Carry water, particularly from May to September.
  • Bring a light jacket for winter evenings and desert nights.
  • Book a private guide and driver to move at your own pace and avoid the hottest hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best month to visit Egypt?

October, November, March and April are a good balance of pleasant weather and manageable crowds. December and January are the most popular months.

Is Egypt too hot in summer?

Cairo and Upper Egypt are hot from May to September. You can still sightsee by starting early in the morning, resting in the middle of the day and enjoying evening activities.

Is winter a good time to visit Cairo?

Yes. Days are mild and comfortable for walking, though mornings and evenings can be cool. It is also the busiest season, so book ahead.

When is the quietest time to visit Egypt?

Summer and the periods around late October, November, March and April usually have fewer visitors than the December to January peak.

Can I visit during Ramadan?

Yes. Major sites stay open, though hours may change and some places open later in the day. Evenings are lively after sunset.

When should I go to the White Desert?

The cooler months from October to March are the most comfortable for the daytime, but nights can be cold, so bring warm clothing for camping.
