Alexandria Highlights: Qaitbay Citadel, Catacombs and the Library

5 min read

Alexandria is Egypt's Mediterranean city, about 3 hours from Cairo, and it is very different from the capital. These are the highlights most visitors plan around, and what each one is like.

Quick answer: For one day in Alexandria, most travellers see three places: Qaitbay Citadel on the sea, the Catacombs of Kom El Shoqafa, and the Bibliotheca Alexandrina. With a private driver from Cairo the full day is about 11 hours including the drive.

Qaitbay Citadel

Qaitbay Citadel stands at the edge of the harbour, built in the 15th century by Sultan Qaitbay on the site of the ancient Pharos Lighthouse. You can walk through the fort and look out over the Mediterranean, which makes it one of the best photo stops in the city.

Catacombs of Kom El Shoqafa

These Roman-era tombs, cut deep into the rock, mix Egyptian, Greek and Roman styles in the carvings. Visitors go down a spiral staircase to the burial chambers. It is one of the most interesting ancient sites in Alexandria, and it is quite different from the pyramids.

Bibliotheca Alexandrina

The Bibliotheca Alexandrina is a modern library and cultural centre on the waterfront, opened in 2002 in honour of the ancient Library of Alexandria. Its tilted circular roof and carved stone wall are worth seeing even if you do not go in for long.

Pompey's Pillar and Other Stops

Pompey's Pillar is a tall Roman column near the Catacombs, and it fits easily into the same visit. Many travellers also stop for lunch by the sea, with fresh fish, before the drive back.

How Much Time Do You Need?

Plan a full day. The drive is about 3 hours each way, and each site needs around an hour. Starting early gives you time for lunch and an unhurried return.

Tips for the Day

  • Wear comfortable shoes, as the Citadel and the Catacombs involve walking and stairs.
  • Bring a light jacket in winter, as the sea breeze can be cool.
  • Carry a little cash in Egyptian pounds for small purchases.
  • Summer is hot, so bring water and a hat.
