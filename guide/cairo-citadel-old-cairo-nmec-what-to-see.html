Cairo Citadel, Old Cairo & NMEC: What to See

4 min read

Cairo is more than the pyramids. This day shows the city's Islamic and Coptic heritage and its newest museum. Here is what to expect and who it suits best.

The Short Answer

This is a good day for travelers who have seen, or plan to see, Giza and want to understand the living city and the centuries that came after the pharaohs. It pairs very well with a pyramids day, because it covers a completely different side of Cairo.

What You Will See

  • The Citadel of Saladin: built in the 12th century on a hill overlooking the city. It houses the Alabaster Mosque of Muhammad Ali and offers sweeping views over Cairo.
  • Old Cairo: narrow streets with some of the country's oldest churches and historic mosques, a calm contrast to the busy modern city.
  • The National Museum of Egyptian Civilization (NMEC): the museum where 22 royal mummies were moved in a widely covered procession in 2021. It also traces Egyptian history from prehistory through to the present day.

Why the Museum Is Worth Your Time

NMEC is a good complement to the Grand Egyptian Museum. Instead of focusing on the pharaohs' treasures, it tells the story of Egypt across many periods, and it lets you see royal mummies in a modern setting.

Who Is It For?

  • Good fit: travelers with a few days in Cairo, history lovers, and anyone who wants to see more than the pyramids.
  • Maybe skip it: if you only have one day in Egypt and want the pyramids first.

Tips for the Day

  • Dress modestly. You will visit mosques and churches, so cover your shoulders and knees. Bring a light scarf if you wish.
  • Wear comfortable shoes. There is a lot of walking, including on uneven streets and hills.
  • Start early. The Citadel is more pleasant before the midday heat.
  • Go private if you can. A guide explains the history behind the sites, and a driver saves time between stops across a busy city.
