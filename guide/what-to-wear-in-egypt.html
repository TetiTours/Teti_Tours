What to Wear in Egypt: A Practical Guide

5 min read

Dressing well in Egypt is about three things: comfort in the sun, respect for local customs, and being ready for sand and long walks. Here is a simple guide by situation and season.

Quick answer: Wear light, breathable clothes that cover shoulders and knees, comfortable closed or sturdy walking shoes, and sun protection. Bring a light jacket or scarf for evenings and the desert, and carry something to cover up if you plan to enter a mosque.

The Basic Rule

Egypt is a conservative country in many places, and covered shoulders and knees are comfortable in the sun and respectful in local areas. Tourist resorts are more relaxed, but for sightseeing in Cairo and around the sites, light and modest clothes are the practical choice for everyone.

For Sightseeing in Cairo and Giza

  • Light cotton or linen tops and trousers, or longer skirts and dresses.
  • Comfortable walking shoes, since you will walk on sand, stone and uneven ground.
  • A hat or cap, sunglasses and sunscreen.
  • A small bag or backpack with water.

For Mosques and Religious Sites

Visitors should dress modestly. Shoulders and knees should be covered, and women are usually asked to cover their hair at active mosques. A light scarf is easy to carry for this. You will also remove your shoes, so slip-on shoes and clean socks are practical.

By Season

  • October to April: light layers, with a jacket or sweater for mornings and evenings.
  • May to September: the lightest, most breathable fabrics you have, plus a hat and plenty of water. See best time to visit Egypt for the weather.

For the Desert

Desert days are warm and nights cool or cold, especially in winter. Wear layers you can add or remove, sturdy shoes, a scarf for sun and dust, and warm clothes for camping. Our guide on the White Desert day trip or overnight camping has more tips.

For Evening Activities

For a Nile dinner cruise or a Bedouin dinner, smart casual is comfortable. Bring a light jacket, since it can be breezy on the water and cool in the desert after sunset.

Things Worth Packing

  • Sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat.
  • A reusable water bottle.
  • A light scarf or shawl.
  • A small first aid kit and any personal medicines.
  • A power bank for your phone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should women wear in Egypt?

Light, loose clothing that covers shoulders and knees is comfortable and respectful, with a scarf for mosques and sun.

What should men wear in Egypt?

Light trousers or longer shorts and a breathable shirt, with comfortable walking shoes. Cover up for mosques.

Can I wear shorts in Egypt?

In the heat, many visitors wear them at sites. Knee-length or longer is more comfortable and respectful, and required covering is expected at mosques.

Do I need a scarf?

A light scarf is useful for sun, dust, and entering mosques.

What shoes should I bring?

Comfortable walking shoes or sturdy sandals, since many sites involve sand, steps and uneven ground.

What should I wear in the desert?

Layers, sturdy shoes, sun protection and a warm jacket for the night, especially from late autumn to early spring. For a desert trip, see our White and Black Desert tour.
