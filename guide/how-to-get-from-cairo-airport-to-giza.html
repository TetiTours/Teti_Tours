How to Get from Cairo Airport to Giza

4 min read

Landing at Cairo International Airport and heading to Giza? Here's everything you need to know about getting from the airport to your hotel near the Pyramids — distance, travel time, and the best transport options.

How Far Is Giza from Cairo Airport?

Giza is located about 35–45 km from Cairo International Airport, depending on your exact hotel location. The trip usually takes 45 minutes to 1 hour, but can extend to 1.5 hours during peak traffic hours (especially in the afternoon).

Transport Options from the Airport

  • Private transfer: The most comfortable and reliable option — a driver meets you at arrivals and takes you directly to your hotel, no waiting or negotiating.
  • Airport taxis: Available but require price negotiation and can vary widely in quality and reliability.
  • Ride-hailing apps: Uber and Careem operate in Cairo, though pickup coordination at the airport can be inconsistent.

What to Expect on Arrival

After clearing customs, you'll exit into the arrivals hall. If you've booked a private transfer, your driver will be waiting with a sign showing your name. It's a straightforward, stress-free way to start your trip to Egypt.
