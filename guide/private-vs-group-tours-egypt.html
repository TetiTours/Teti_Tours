Private vs Group Tours in Egypt: Which Is Better?

5 min read

One of the first choices when planning a trip to Egypt is whether to join a group tour or book a private one. Both can work well. This guide explains the differences so you can pick what suits your group.

Quick answer: A private tour gives you your own guide and driver, hotel pickup and a flexible schedule, which suits families, couples and anyone who wants to move at their own pace. A group tour shares the vehicle and schedule with other travellers, with a fixed itinerary and timings. The right choice depends on how much flexibility, comfort and time you want.

What Is a Private Tour?

On a private tour, the vehicle, driver and guide are for you and the people travelling with you. You are collected from your hotel at a time that suits you, and the day is built around your interests rather than a fixed programme.

What Is a Group Tour?

A group tour brings together travellers who have booked separately and share one vehicle and one guide. The route, stops and timings are set in advance, and the group moves together. Group sizes vary from small to large depending on the operator.

Main Differences

  • Flexibility: a private tour lets you spend longer where you like and skip what you do not. A group tour follows a set schedule.
  • Pace: private tours move at your speed, with time for photos, breaks and questions. Group tours move at the speed of the whole group.
  • Pickup: private tours collect you from your hotel at an agreed time. Group tours may collect several travellers on a route, which can add waiting time.
  • Comfort: private vehicles are sized for your party. Shared vehicles are fuller.
  • Company: group tours are a way to meet other travellers. Private tours are just you and your own party.

Who Suits a Private Tour?

  • Families with children, who may need breaks, changes of pace or flexible timings.
  • Couples looking for a relaxed, personal day.
  • Travellers with limited time who want to see as much as possible in a day.
  • Anyone interested in photography, history or particular sites.
  • Visitors who prefer to avoid the hottest hours or the busiest times. See our guide on the best time to visit Egypt.

Who Suits a Group Tour?

  • Solo travellers who like meeting other people.
  • Travellers happy to follow a fixed programme.
  • Visitors comfortable with set timings and a shared vehicle.

Private or Shared for Long Desert Trips

For longer trips, such as the White and Black Desert, the choice can matter even more. The journey is long, the days are full, and the group shares a vehicle for many hours. Some travellers prefer a shared trip for the company, while others choose private for flexibility and comfort. Our guide on the White Desert day trip or overnight camping explains what to expect.

Questions to Ask Before You Book

  • Is the tour private, or will I share the vehicle?
  • Is hotel pickup and drop-off included?
  • What is included, such as entrance tickets, lunch and the guide?
  • How long will the day be, and can the schedule change?
  • What is the cancellation policy?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a private tour better than a group tour in Egypt?

It depends on your priorities. A private tour offers flexibility, comfort and hotel pickup at a time you choose. A group tour follows a fixed schedule and can be a way to meet other travellers. Browse our private Giza tour as an example.

Can I choose my own pickup time on a private tour?

Yes. Private tours are usually arranged around a pickup time that suits you, within the tour's schedule.

Are private tours good for families with children?

Yes. A private tour makes it easier to take breaks, change the pace and manage timings around children.

Do private tours cover the same sites as group tours?

Yes. The sites are the same. The difference is how you visit them, with your own schedule and vehicle.

Should I book a private tour for the White and Black Desert?

Either can work. Shared trips suit travellers who like company, while private trips offer more flexibility and comfort on a long journey. See our White and Black Desert tour.

How far ahead should I book?

Booking a few days ahead is usually enough for most tours, and earlier is better in busy months such as December and January.
