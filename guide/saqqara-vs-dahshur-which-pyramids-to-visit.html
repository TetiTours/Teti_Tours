Saqqara vs Dahshur: Which Pyramids to Visit?

4 min read

Many travelers see the Giza Pyramids and want more, and then ask whether to choose Saqqara or Dahshur. The honest answer is that they show different chapters of the same story, and you can often see both in one day.

The Short Answer

Choose Saqqara if you want the first pyramid and the tombs with beautifully preserved wall carvings. Choose Dahshur if you want a quiet visit and a look at how pyramid builders learned by trial and error. If you have a full day, do both and add Memphis.

Saqqara

  • The Step Pyramid of Djoser: built around 2670 BC, it is Egypt's first pyramid and the world's oldest large stone building. Every later pyramid evolved from this design.
  • The tombs: the site also holds tombs with vividly preserved carvings on the walls, showing daily life and religious scenes.
  • Best for: anyone who wants to understand how the pyramids began.

Dahshur

  • The Bent Pyramid and the Red Pyramid: two pyramids that show the architects' early experiments in getting the angles right. The Red Pyramid has smooth sides, while the Bent Pyramid changes angle partway up.
  • A quieter setting: Dahshur is far less crowded than Giza, so it is easier to take your time and take photos.
  • Best for: travelers who prefer space and quiet, and who enjoy seeing how a design developed.

Memphis

Memphis was ancient Egypt's first capital. Today its open-air museum holds a huge fallen statue of Ramesses II and an alabaster sphinx, which give you a sense of how large the city once was. It is a short, easy stop that pairs well with both sites.

How to Combine Them

  • Saqqara, Dahshur and Memphis: the best choice if the pyramids' history is what interests you. You trace the pyramid from its first steps to the smooth sides.
  • Giza, Saqqara and Memphis: a good choice if you have not yet seen the Great Pyramid and the Sphinx, and want one day that covers the famous sights plus the early ones.

Tips for the Day

  • Start early. It is cooler and quieter, and a day with several sites needs time.
  • Wear comfortable shoes and sun protection. These are open desert sites with little shade.
  • Bring water. You will be outdoors for much of the day.
  • Go private if you can. A guide and a car let you move between sites at your own pace and spend longer where you like.
