Is Cairo Safe for Tourists? Practical Tips

5 min read

Many travellers ask whether Cairo is safe before they book. Millions of visitors enjoy Egypt each year, and a little awareness helps you enjoy it too. This guide covers the practical side: common hassles, getting around and sensible habits.

Quick answer: Cairo is a large, busy city that welcomes many visitors every year. Most issues tourists run into are everyday annoyances such as persistent sellers, unclear taxi prices and crowds, rather than serious problems. Use sensible precautions, arrange transport in advance and always check your government's current travel advice before you go.

Check Current Travel Advice

Conditions can change, so before you travel, read the current guidance from your own government's foreign travel advice service. It is the best source for up-to-date information.

The Everyday Reality

Cairo is busy, loud and full of life. Most visitors find that the most common problems are not dangerous, but tiring: heavy traffic, crowds, and sellers who may approach you near popular sites. A calm, polite "no, thank you" and continuing to walk is usually enough.

Common Tourist Hassles

  • Persistent sellers and offers of help: near major sites, people may offer rides, photos or souvenirs. Decline politely if you are not interested.
  • Unclear prices: agree prices before you accept a ride, a camel or horse, or a service.
  • Unexpected stops: some drivers may suggest shops or other stops. You can say you want to keep to your plan.
  • Crowds: keep your phone and wallet secure in busy places.

Getting Around Safely

Traffic is the main challenge in Cairo. Many visitors use a private driver or arranged transfer, which means fixed arrangements and no need to negotiate. If you use ride-hailing apps, check the car and driver details before you get in. For arrivals, see our guide on how to get from Cairo Airport to Giza.

Staying Comfortable and Respectful

  • Dress modestly, which is also more comfortable in the sun.
  • Drink bottled water and carry sun protection.
  • Ask before photographing people.
  • Keep valuables out of sight and bags closed in crowds.
  • Share your plans with your hotel and keep their number handy.

Solo and Female Travellers

Many solo and female travellers visit Egypt and have a great trip. Some report unwanted attention in busy areas. Dressing modestly, using arranged transport, especially at night, and travelling with a trusted guide or driver can make the experience more relaxed.

Why a Private Guide Helps

A guide who knows the sites handles tickets and timings, deals with sellers on your behalf and helps you avoid common hassles, so you can focus on the sights. It also makes first visits much easier. See our Egypt travel tips for first-time visitors for more practical advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cairo safe for tourists?

Millions of visitors travel to Egypt each year. Cairo is busy, and common issues are everyday hassles like persistent sellers and unclear prices. Check your government's current travel advice and use sensible precautions.

Is it safe to walk around Cairo?

Many visitors do, particularly in busy daytime areas. Traffic is a concern, so cross carefully, and keep valuables secure in crowds.

How do I avoid scams in Cairo?

Agree prices in advance, be polite but firm about declining offers, and use arranged transport or a trusted driver.

Is it safe for solo female travellers?

Many women travel in Egypt successfully. Modest dress, arranged transport at night and a trusted guide can help the experience feel more comfortable.

Is it safe to visit the Pyramids?

Yes, the Giza Pyramids are one of the most visited sites in the world. Expect sellers near the entrance and agree any extra activities in advance.

Is the water safe to drink?

Visitors usually drink bottled or filtered water.
