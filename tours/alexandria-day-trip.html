Alexandria Day Trip

Book this tour ↓

Alexandria was founded by Alexander the Great in 331 BC and later became one of the ancient world's great centers of learning, home to its famous Great Library. Today the Mediterranean coastal city blends that history with a breezy, European-influenced atmosphere along its corniche. The tour visits the Catacombs of Kom El Shoqafa, an underground necropolis mixing Egyptian, Greek and Roman styles; Qaitbay Citadel, a 15th-century fortress built on the site of the ancient Lighthouse of Alexandria, one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World; and the modern Bibliotheca Alexandrina, a striking library built in tribute to the original.

Do I need a visa to visit Egypt?

Most visitors need a visa, which can usually be arranged online in advance (e-visa) or on arrival at the airport. Requirements vary by nationality, so check the current rules for your country on the official Egypt e-Visa Portal before you travel.

What currency is used in Egypt?

The Egyptian Pound (EGP) is the official currency, though USD and EUR are widely accepted in tourist areas. It's a good idea to carry some local currency for smaller purchases.

Is tipping customary in Egypt?

Yes, tipping, locally known as "baksheesh," is common for good service. The amount is entirely up to you and depends on the quality of service. Tipping your guide and driver is appreciated but always optional.

Is Egypt safe for tourists?

Egypt is a popular destination for tourists, and millions of visitors enjoy its historical sites and cultural experiences every year. As with any destination, it's wise to stay aware of your surroundings, follow local guidance, and keep valuables secure.

What's the best time to visit Egypt?

October to April offers the most comfortable weather for sightseeing, though Egypt can be visited year-round — summers are hot but still popular, especially for coastal areas.
